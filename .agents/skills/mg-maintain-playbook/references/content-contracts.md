# Actionable page contracts

Use these contracts when drafting, revising, or reviewing actionable pages. They guide page structure; they do not adopt policy. Follow existing neighboring formats where useful. For every field, record only established facts; label unknowns and decisions as open rather than inventing them.

For new or substantially revised actionable pages, include a stable ID, title, type, accountable team or role, status, applicability, and last-reviewed date when relevant and confirmed. Use a local format if established; otherwise use a compact metadata block. Suggested statuses are `draft`, `active`, and `deprecated`; never mark a draft active. A review date does not prove execution or verification.

- **Play:** Trigger, outcome, responsible roles, prerequisites, ordered actions and decisions, completion evidence, and failure or escalation route.
- **Standard:** Rule; required, recommended, or context-dependent classification; rationale, applicability, examples, and exception mechanism. Distinguish adopted guidance from a proposal awaiting a decision.
- **Recipe or runbook:** Environment/version, inputs, prerequisites and required access, ordered usable steps, expected results, verification, and known failure recovery. Record recipe last-verified date and evidence separately from last-reviewed. Include only commands and access routes verified for the stated environment. Missing procedure, recovery, or evidence is a draft gap.
- **Reference:** Explanation, scope, authoritative sources, and links to applicable workflows.
- **Learning page:** Competency, exercise, observable learning evidence, and links to relevant standards.
- **Skill:** Activation conditions, required context, procedure, tool/access expectations, verification, and output contract.
